Welcome to Big God Church

At Big God International Revival Ministries – Nimule, we believe the Church is more than a building. It is a spiritual family where people encounter God, discover purpose, and find hope in every season of life.

Through worship, biblical teaching, prayer, discipleship, and compassionate outreach, we are committed to raising spiritually grounded believers and transforming communities through the love of Christ.

Whether you are searching for a church home, seeking encouragement, or desiring a deeper relationship with God, you are welcome here.

Our Foundation

“For where two or three gather in my name, there am I with them.” — Matthew 18:20

Everything we do is rooted in the Word of God, guided by prayer, and centered on Jesus Christ. Our desire is to see hearts restored, families strengthened, and communities transformed through the Gospel.
