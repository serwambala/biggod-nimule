Restoring Hope Through Faith

“Defend the weak and the fatherless; uphold the cause of the poor and the oppressed.” — Psalm 82:3

Many children in Nimule, South Sudan, face hardship, displacement, and limited access to education and spiritual support. At Big God International Revival Ministries, through prayer, community outreach, mentorship and compassion, we are committed to restoring hope. We believe every child in Nimule deserves dignity and a future.
