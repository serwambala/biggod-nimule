Our Story

A journey of faith, compassion, and restoration in Nimule, South Sudan.

How the Ministry Began

Big God International Revival Ministries - Nimule is a branch of Big God International Revival Ministries - Juba under the overseership of bishop Bishop Joseph Mutazibwa. We bless the Lord for him

Big God International Revival Ministries was born out of a deep burden to serve God faithfully while reaching vulnerable communities with the love of Christ.

In Nimule, many families and children continue to face hardship caused by displacement, poverty, limited educational opportunities, and spiritual struggles. What began as prayer, fellowship, and outreach slowly grew into a ministry dedicated to bringing both spiritual transformation and practical support to the community.

Through worship, discipleship, mentorship, and humanitarian outreach, the ministry continues to shine the light of Christ in the lives of children, families, and believers in Nimule.

Guided By Faith

Our Journey

Prayer & Fellowship

The ministry began with prayer gatherings, worship, and a passion for spiritual revival within the community.

Community Outreach

As needs became more visible, outreach efforts expanded to support struggling families and vulnerable children.

Children Support

Through mentorship, care, and compassion, the ministry continues to restore dignity and hope to children in Nimule.

The Heart Behind the Ministry

At the center of this ministry is a commitment to serve with humility, compassion, and unwavering faith in God.

We believe the Gospel is not only preached through words, but also demonstrated through acts of love, care, encouragement, and service to the vulnerable.

Every prayer, every outreach effort, and every act of support is part of a greater mission to restore hope and point lives toward Christ.
