Statement of Faith

What we believe, teach, and stand upon as a ministry rooted in the Word of God.

Our Foundation

Our beliefs are grounded in the Holy Scriptures, which we accept as the inspired and authoritative Word of God. Everything we do as a ministry flows from these truths.

This statement of faith guides our teaching, worship, leadership, and outreach as we serve communities in Nimule and beyond.

What We Believe

The Bible

We believe the Bible is the inspired, infallible Word of God, and the final authority in all matters of faith and life.

God (The Trinity)

We believe in one God, eternally existing in three persons: Father, Son, and Holy Spirit.

Jesus Christ

We believe Jesus Christ is the Son of God, fully divine and fully human, who died for our sins and rose again.

Salvation

We believe salvation is a gift of grace through faith in Jesus Christ alone, not by works.

The Holy Spirit

We believe the Holy Spirit empowers believers to live godly lives, serve effectively, and grow spiritually.

The Church

We believe the Church is the body of Christ, called to worship God, disciple believers, and reach the world.

Prayer

We believe prayer is essential for communion with God, guidance, healing, and spiritual strength.

Eternal Life

We believe in eternal life through Jesus Christ, with resurrection and judgment for all.

Our Anchor

“All Scripture is God-breathed and is useful for teaching, rebuking, correcting and training in righteousness.” — 2 Timothy 3:16

How This Shapes Our Ministry

Our faith is not theoretical—it is lived out daily through worship, service, discipleship, and outreach.

Every program, prayer gathering, and act of compassion is guided by these beliefs as we seek to glorify God and serve people faithfully.

We remain committed to preaching the Gospel of Jesus Christ and demonstrating His love through action.

Stand With Us in Faith

Join us as we grow in faith, serve communities, and proclaim the Gospel of Jesus Christ in Nimule and beyond.
