About Us

Big God International Revival Ministries – Nimule is a faith-based ministry committed to preaching the Gospel of Jesus Christ and supporting vulnerable children and communities in Nimule, South Sudan.

Bishop Mutazibwa Joseph is the founding father of Big God International Revival Ministries. He is currently based in Juba South Sudan. We thank God for his visionary leadership and the annointing upon his life. Big God Church - Nimule is therefore overseen by Bishop Joseph Mutazibwa.

Our mission is to bring spiritual transformation through the Church and practical compassion through children’s outreach programs including education support, care, and mentorship.

We believe that every child deserves hope, dignity, and a future filled with purpose.
