Vision & Mission

Advancing the Gospel, restoring hope, and transforming lives through faith, compassion, and community outreach.

Our Calling

Big God International Revival Ministries exists to proclaim the love of Christ while serving communities through worship, discipleship, prayer, mentorship, and humanitarian outreach.

We are committed to nurturing spiritual growth, strengthening families, supporting vulnerable children, and raising a generation rooted in faith, compassion, and hope.

✨

Our Vision

To see transformed lives, empowered communities, and spiritually grounded generations through the life-changing power of Jesus Christ.

We envision a ministry that shines the light of God across Nimule and beyond through worship, love, outreach, and compassionate service.

❤️

Our Mission

To preach the Gospel, disciple believers, care for vulnerable children and families, and serve communities through faith-centered outreach.

Through prayer, teaching, mentorship, and humanitarian support, we seek to restore dignity, hope, and purpose in the lives of those we serve.

What Drives Our Ministry

Spiritual Growth

Teaching God’s Word, strengthening faith, and nurturing believers through discipleship and worship.

Compassionate Outreach

Demonstrating Christ’s love through practical support, mentorship, and community care initiatives.

Children & Families

Supporting vulnerable children and families with encouragement, hope, and opportunities for growth.

Community Transformation

Building stronger communities rooted in faith, unity, peace, and spiritual revival.
