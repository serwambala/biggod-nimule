Why We Serve Children

Many children in Nimule face poverty, displacement, limited access to education, trauma, and spiritual neglect. Through Big God International Revival Ministries, we are committed to raising a generation filled with hope, dignity, and purpose.

Our ministry provides practical care, spiritual mentorship, educational support, feeding programs, and safe community environments where children can grow and thrive.

“Defend the weak and the fatherless; uphold the cause of the poor and oppressed.” — Psalm 82:3

What We Provide

Education Support

School fees assistance, school supplies, mentorship, and academic encouragement.

Feeding Programs

Providing meals and nutritional support to vulnerable children and families.

Spiritual Mentorship

Teaching children the Word of God, prayer, worship, and Christian values.

Medical Assistance

Helping children access basic medical care, medication, and wellness support.

Safe Community

Creating safe spaces where children experience love, encouragement, and belonging.

Clothing & Essentials

Providing clothes, hygiene supplies, and emergency child assistance.
