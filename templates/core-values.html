Our Core Values

The principles that guide our ministry, shape our service, and define our commitment to God and the community.

What We Stand For

At Big God International Revival Ministries, our values are rooted in Scripture and reflected in how we worship, serve, lead, and care for others.

These principles shape every outreach effort, ministry activity, prayer gathering, and act of compassion.

Our Guiding Principles

🙏

Faith in God

We believe in trusting God fully, seeking His guidance in every aspect of ministry and life.

❤️

Compassion

We are committed to serving vulnerable children, families, and communities with love and dignity.

📖

Biblical Truth

God’s Word is the foundation of our teaching, discipleship, and spiritual growth.

🤝

Service

We believe ministry is demonstrated through humble service, encouragement, and practical support.

🌍

Community Impact

We desire to see transformed lives, restored hope, and stronger communities.

✨

Integrity

We strive to walk in honesty, accountability, and faithfulness before God and people.

Our Foundation Scripture

“Whatever you do, work at it with all your heart, as working for the Lord.” — Colossians 3:23

Living Out Our Values

Our values are not simply statements on a page. They are reflected in worship, outreach, mentorship, prayer, and compassionate service.

Through every ministry effort, we seek to reflect the character of Christ while bringing hope and encouragement to those in need.

We believe true ministry happens when faith, love, integrity, and service work together.

Walk With Us

Join us as we continue building a ministry rooted in faith, compassion, truth, and service.
